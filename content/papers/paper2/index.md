##### Abstract

Using the Lee-Yang zeros to explain the origin of the fermion sign problem, we analyze the movement of the zeros at finite temperature with analytically solvable non-interacting systems, and propose a method to obtain the low-temperature properties of fermionic systems—severely affected by the fermion sign problem—by analytic continuation of high-temperature thermodynamic results on the complex plane. This paper is Part II of a series revisiting the fermion sign problem from the structure of Lee-Yang zeros.

**Status:** submitted to *Physical Review E*. [arXiv:2606.07415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07415)
