##### Abstract

We develop an efficient integrator scheme—the middle scheme—for sampling the (quantum) isobaric–isothermal ensemble in (path integral) molecular dynamics simulations. By adjusting the order of the thermostat and barostat coupling, the scheme allows enlarged sampling time steps in both classical and path integral molecular dynamics.
