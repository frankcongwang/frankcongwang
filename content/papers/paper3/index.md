##### Abstract

We connect the fermion sign problem (FSP) with the Lee-Yang theory of phase transitions, extend the symmetry parameter $\xi$ (bosons $\xi=1$, fermions $\xi=-1$) to the complex plane, and discover that the partition function of indistinguishable particles is a polynomial in $\xi$, so that all systems possess fixed zeros at zero temperature in the $\xi$ space that depend only on the particle number. This paper is Part I of a series revisiting the fermion sign problem from the structure of Lee-Yang zeros.

Together, the three papers of this series establish a theoretical framework for studying the FSP via Lee-Yang theory, explain the limitations of current path-integral-based fermionic methods, and guide the development of methods for solving the fermion sign problem.
