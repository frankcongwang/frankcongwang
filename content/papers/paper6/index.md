##### Abstract

We analyze the stationary state distribution and sampling efficiency of the Langevin equation and its variants through real or virtual dynamics, and propose the middle scheme thermostat algorithm, which enlarges the sampling time step by adjusting the order of the thermostat coupling. The algorithm has been widely adopted by software packages such as Amber.
